# SLIDE 4: DOMAIN OVERVIEW & PROBLEM STATEMENT
Slide 4: Domain Overview & Problem Statement
- Primary Domains: Artificial Intelligence (NLP), HR Technology (RecTech), Cloud & Full-Stack Computing.
- Industry Challenges in Manual Recruitment:
  * Extreme Application Volume: Recruiters spend 6–10 seconds per resume; top openings receive hundreds of applicants.
  * Cognitive Fatigue & Inconsistency: Manual screening introduces subjective bias, missed qualifications, and human error.
  * Black-Box AI Limitations: Enterprise ATS systems often reject candidates without auditable explanations or transparent rationale.
- Proposed Solution:
  * An automated, explainable, and deterministic AI recruitment platform that ingests unstructured PDF resumes.
  * Extracts candidate attributes (skills, experience, education, contact details).
  * Benchmarks qualifications against job descriptions and generates transparent scoring analytics.

Slide 5: Engineering Knowledge & Operational Procedures

# SLIDE 6: DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT OF SOLUTIONS & ARCHITECTURE
Slide 6: Design & Development of Solutions & Process Structure
- System Architecture:
  * Presentation Layer: Modern Glassmorphism UI, real-time candidate search, dynamic status filtering, and score breakdown cards.
  * Application & Logic Engine: Flask backend, PyPDF stream extraction, entity extractors, taxonomy matcher, and scoring calculator.
  * Persistence Layer: Relational candidate and job storage, audit logging, designed for Azure SQL / Blob migration.
- Explainable Multi-Factor Scoring Model:
  * Composite Formula: Overall Score = (0.50 * Skill Score) + (0.25 * Experience Score) + (0.25 * Education Score)
  * Skill Score: (Matched Skills / Required Skills) * 100
  * Experience Score: min(100, (Candidate Experience / Required Experience) * 100)
  * Education Score: 100% on degree requirement match, with support for degree hierarchy (Ph.D, M.Tech, B.Tech, MCA, BCA, etc.).
- Recruitment Workflow:
  * Job Creation -> Bulk PDF Ingestion -> Text & Entity Extraction -> Weighted Scoring -> Recruiter Dashboard & Status Updates (Shortlisted / Under Review / Rejected).

# SLIDE 9: SOCIETAL & ENVIRONMENTAL CONCERNS
Slide 9: Societal & Environmental Concerns
- Societal Impact & Ethical AI:
  * Elimination of Unconscious Bias: Evaluates applicants strictly on verified skills, years of experience, and degrees—eliminating gender, demographic, and appearance bias.
  * Transparent & Auditable Decisions: Replaces opaque "black-box" rejections with clear matched/missing criteria breakdowns.
